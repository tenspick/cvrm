Academic Divisions

Centers of Scholarly Power

Our four dedicated departments deliver top-tier instruction, combine scientific inquiry with professional practice, and provide advanced resources for scholarly pursuits.

Department of Distinction

Commerce

The Department of Commerce is dedicated to fostering commercial knowledge, accounting competence, and economic reasoning. By combining conceptual coursework with seminars on taxation, auditing, and corporate laws, we prepare students for competitive credentials like CA, CS, and corporate finance roles.

Advanced accounting modeling labs
Seminars on Goods & Services Tax (GST)
Regular audit training sessions
Mock financial audits
Center of Leadership

Management

Our Department of Management focuses on cultivating leadership strategies, human relations competency, marketing foresight, and operational excellence. We foster analytical approaches, strategic reasoning, and communication skills to shape future managers and innovative entrepreneurs.

Case-study based instructional method
Executive guest speaker lectures
Incubation center for student startups
Annual management conclaves
Hub of Scientific Inquiry

Sciences

The Department of Sciences delivers comprehensive research-led instructions in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Botany, and Zoology. Housing state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, our students receive hands-on instruction, conducting advanced experiments and developing algorithmic software models.

Fully-equipped Botany & Chemistry labs
High-speed Computer Science laboratory
Scientific research paper guidance
Regular field visits and reports
School of Humanities

Arts

The Department of Arts provides students with strong liberal arts foundations. Covering history, political models, sociology, and English literature, the department challenges students to think critically, communicate articulately, and prepare for civil service examinations and public administration roles.

Civil service coaching seminars
Debating & literary clubs
Social field survey projects
Creative writing workshops
