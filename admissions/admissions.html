Four Simple Stages

How to Apply

Our admission workflow is designed to be streamlined, transparent, and completely merit-centric.

01

Registration

Register online through our admissions portal or visit our campus office to obtain a registration form.

02

Form Submission

Submit the completed application form along with academic transcripts (10th, Intermediate/12th grade).

03

Counseling & Review

Attend our diagnostic counseling session where our academic advisors review your aspirations and verify eligibility.

04

Enrollment

Upon selection, pay the semester registration fees, submit original documents, and finalize your enrollment.
