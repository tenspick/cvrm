B.Sc Honors (Botany)

Comprehensive knowledge of plant biology, ecology, and applied botany � equipping students for careers in agriculture, forestry, biotechnology, and environmental management.

Program Overview

The B.Sc Honors in Botany provides a rigorous academic grounding in the science of plants � from cellular and molecular biology to ecosystem ecology. Students explore the vast diversity of plant life, understand physiological processes, and apply botanical knowledge to real-world challenges in food security, medicine, and environmental conservation.

With hands-on laboratory training, field excursions, and applied projects, graduates are well-prepared for careers in research institutions, agricultural organizations, pharmaceutical industries, and government agencies.

Core Curriculum

Plant Anatomy & Morphology

Internal structure, tissues, organs, and growth patterns of plants including flowering and non-flowering species.

Plant Physiology & Biochemistry

Photosynthesis, respiration, plant hormones, mineral nutrition, and water relations.

Economic Botany & Ethnobotany

Commercially important plants, medicinal herbs, traditional plant knowledge, and agro-forestry.

Plant Biotechnology & Genetics

Tissue culture, genetic engineering of plants, DNA markers, and genetically modified crops.

Ecology & Environmental Science

Ecosystems, biodiversity conservation, environmental pollution, and climate change impact on flora.

Mycology & Phycology

Study of fungi, algae, lichens, their ecology, economic importance, and biotechnological applications.

Career Pathways & Opportunities

  • Agricultural Officer / Botanist
  • Forestry & Conservation Scientist
  • Biotechnology Research Scientist
  • Pharmaceutical Botanist
  • Environmental Consultant
  • M.Sc Botany / Plant Science
