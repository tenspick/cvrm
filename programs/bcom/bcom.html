Undergraduate Program

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

Build strong financial, accounting, taxation, and auditing competencies, ensuring readiness for the global corporate landscape.

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Commerce is a premium undergraduate program designed to build strong financial, accounting, taxation, and auditing competencies. It prepares students for modern commercial operations, corporate management, and global financial careers.

Through practical case studies, internships, and mentoring from experienced professionals, students acquire both theoretical rigor and real-world execution capacity.

Core Curriculum

Financial Accounting

In-depth understanding of corporate accounting principles, reporting standards, and auditing procedures.

Taxation & Law

Comprehensive studies in direct and indirect taxation, company law, and business regulations.

Management Accounting

Strategic financial management, budgeting, cost accounting, and variance analysis.

Business Economics

Macro and microeconomic analysis, statistical methods, and quantitative forecasting techniques.

Career Pathways & Opportunities

Graduates of the B.Com program have excellent career opportunities in accounting, finance, banking, insurance, and corporate sectors:

  • Chartered Accountant (CA) / CS
  • Financial Analyst
  • Auditor & Corporate Accountant
  • Investment Banker
  • Tax Consultant
  • Financial Risk Manager
