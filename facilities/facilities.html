Our World-Class Spaces

Every corner of our campus is designed to unlock potential, promote scholarship, and build community.

Academic Resource

Library

A silent sanctuary containing over 20,000 reference volumes, journals, and technical archives. Includes digital terminal access points to national database systems and collaboration tables for group study sessions.

  • Over 20,000 print titles and reference books
  • E-Library portal with national database access
  • Quiet individual zones & collaborative areas
Research & Inquiry

Science Laboratories

Dedicated facilities for Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology. The labs house modern reagents, glassware, dissection apparatuses, microscopes, and spectrophotometers to support undergraduate coursework and minor studies.

  • State-of-the-art chemical ventilation hoods
  • High-resolution optical microscopes
  • Strict bio-safety and chemical waste procedures
Computing Power

Computer Lab

Housing high-speed computers loaded with professional programming, database management, and analysis suites. Serves as the primary computational hub for B.Sc and BBA practical instruction.

  • Latest computing nodes with high-speed processors
  • Preloaded compiler suites (Java, Python, C++, SQL)
  • Uninterrupted power back-up system
Interactive Learning

Smart Classrooms

Air-conditioned classrooms equipped with audio systems, interactive whiteboards, and high-lumen projection machinery. Designed to make lectures collaborative, visual, and highly interactive.

  • Interactive multimedia panels & projectors
  • Comfortable ergonomic seating layouts
  • Excellent acoustic designs and lighting control
Physical Development

Sports Facilities

Outdoor playing fields for cricket, football, and running events, alongside dedicated courts for volleyball and basketball. We encourage students to engage in sports to cultivate team coordination and mental resilience.

  • Professional volleyball and basketball courts
  • Annual athletic events & inter-college tournaments
  • Experienced physical education instructors
Career Direction

Career Guidance & Placement Cell

The placement office conducts interviews, mock tests, soft skills seminars, and hosts recruiters. Provides crucial advising to help graduates map out corporate placement strategies and UPSC studies.

  • Mock tests, resume audits & interview prep
  • Recruiter negotiation and hiring schedules
  • Post-grad studies counselling
Campus Connectivity

Transportation

CVRM Degree College operates a reliable fleet of college buses covering major routes across Venkatagirikota and surrounding areas. Our transport service ensures safe, punctual, and comfortable daily commutes for students and staff, reducing travel stress and supporting regular attendance.

  • Multiple bus routes covering key residential zones
  • Punctual pick-up & drop schedules morning and evening
  • Safe, maintained vehicles with experienced drivers
