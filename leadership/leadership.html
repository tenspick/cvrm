Executive Board Messages

Words from Our Leaders

Read about the dedication, principles, and roadmap driving CVRM Degree College's academic achievements.

Founder & Chairman

SHRI. S.R DAMODHAR REDDY

M.Tech, Entrepreneur & Philanthropist

Welcome to CVRM Degree College. Our institution was founded with a singular focus: to deliver premium-tier tertiary education to Venkatagirikota, equipping students with competitive professional competencies and high-character traits.

We believe that education must evolve beyond traditional lecture systems. By investing in smart classrooms, connected laboratories, and career training early, we ensure that CVRM graduates enter corporate systems or competitive public tracks with confidence and integrity.

"True education does not merely construct careers; it transforms potential into legacy."

Institutional Secretary

SHRI CV GANESH

MBA

At CVRM Degree College, we focus on institutional execution. Our administration works hard to make sure that curriculum requirements are paired with industrial software suites, guest lectures, and diagnostic placement counseling starting from the second year.

We are continuously expanding our placement channels and upgrading our computing infrastructure. By keeping our departments connected to modern trends, we build a learning experience that meets corporate recruitment standards.

"Innovation and execution must move in tandem to build sustainable career outcomes."

Academic Principal

DR. M .YUGANDHAR REDDY

Ph.D , M.COM , B.ED & Researcher

Academic rigor and holistic character formation are the core pillars of our college. Under the scientific direction and researcher mindset of Dr. Yugandhar Reddy, CVRM Degree College combines experimental laboratory lessons, interactive seminars, and competitive placement training to nurture future-ready graduates.

"Intellectual curiosity and disciplined study are the primary tools to navigate the modern economy."
